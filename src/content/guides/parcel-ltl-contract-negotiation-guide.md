## The 2027 picture

FedEx set a 5.9% average increase for 2027, its fourth in a row, with service-level increases from about 3.1% (Express Saver) to 6.65% (2Day A.M.) and higher residential and handling surcharges. LTL carriers are raising rates earlier than usual: Old Dominion 4.9% in October 2026, Saia 7.1% in July and ArcBest 5.9% in June. See our coverage of [parcel](/articles/2026-09-21-fedex-2027-rate-increase-5-9-percent-surcharges-parcel/) and [LTL](/articles/2026-09-22-ltl-general-rate-increases-old-dominion-saia-arcbest-2026/) increases.

## Step 1: Know your real increase

Re-rate the last 12 months of shipments using next year's rates:

- By service level, zone and weight band
- By address type (residential or commercial)
- Including every surcharge and accessorial you actually incurred

The result often differs from the headline. Light residential packages tend to rise faster than average; transit-tolerant shipments on economy services rise slower.

## Step 2: Negotiate what drives your bill

**Parcel**

| Term | What to ask for |
|---|---|
| Discounts by service and weight band | Discounts targeted at the weights and zones you ship most |
| Residential and delivery area surcharges | Reductions or waivers based on volume |
| Additional handling and large package | Higher thresholds or discounts |
| Fuel surcharge | A discount off the published table or a cap |
| Minimum charges | Lower minimums for light packages |
| Dimensional weight divisor | A more favorable divisor |
| Earned discount tiers | Tiers that match realistic volume |

**LTL**

| Term | What to ask for |
|---|---|
| Base tariff | A frozen base for the term, or a cap on annual increases |
| Discount and absolute minimum charge | Competitive discount and floor |
| Freight class | Class exceptions (FAK) for your common commodities |
| Accessorials | Fixed rates for liftgate, residential, inside delivery and detention |
| Fuel surcharge | A capped or discounted table |

## Step 3: Fix your data

- **Dimensions and weight:** inaccurate dimensions trigger adjustments and handling charges.
- **Freight class and density:** in LTL, misclassification is a common source of reweigh and reclass charges.
- **Address quality:** correct residential flags avoid surprise surcharges.
- **Paperless shipping:** FedEx is adding fees for paper trade documents and manual airbills in 2027.

## Step 4: Re-bid regularly

Every two to three years, run a structured re-bid with your current carrier, at least one national competitor and regional carriers where your volume is concentrated. Even if you stay, a competitive process typically improves terms.

## Step 5: Audit invoices

Use a parcel and freight audit process to catch service failures eligible for refunds, incorrect surcharges and reweigh adjustments. Recover overcharges monthly.
