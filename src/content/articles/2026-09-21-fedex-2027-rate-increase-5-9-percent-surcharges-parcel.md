## Why it matters

Parcel general rate increases compound. A 5.9% average, four years running, adds up to roughly 26% since 2023, by our calculation, before surcharges, which are rising faster in some cases. Teams that negotiate parcel contracts should look past the headline to the services and fees they actually use.

## The details

FedEx will raise standard U.S. package rates, including import and export, by an average of 5.9% effective Jan. 4, 2027, matching its increases in 2024, 2025 and 2026. Minimum rates and many surcharges rise the same day. Ground Economy, Ground Multiweight, International Premium and International Priority DirectDistribution also rise, by amounts FedEx didn't specify.

**By service** (analysis by Loop, reported by Supply Chain Dive):

| Service | Increase |
|---|---|
| 2Day A.M. | 6.65% |
| FedEx Ground, 1–5 lb | 6.49% |
| First Overnight | 6.01% |
| Standard Overnight | 5.16% |
| Express Saver | 3.09% |

Loop advised shippers with transit-tolerant freight on 2Day to "model the Express Saver alternative before their next renewal."

**Surcharges, 2026 to 2027:**

| Surcharge | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Additional handling, weight-based (U.S., zone 2) | $46.00 | $49.50 |
| Additional handling, dimension-based (international) | $29.50 | $32.25 |
| Residential delivery | $6.95 | $7.35 |
| Delivery area, residential | $6.60 | $7.00 |
| Delivery area, commercial | $4.45 | $4.70 |
| Oversize, Home Delivery zones 3–4 | $275 | $290 |
| U.S. inbound processing, International Ground | $2.65 | $2.80 |

**New fees.** FedEx will add a $25 fee when trade documents are provided on paper rather than electronically, and a $5 fee for shipments started with manual paper airbills, effective Jan. 18.

UPS had not announced its 2027 increase as of Sept. 21, though it has historically matched FedEx closely.

**Surcharges are rising faster than the headline.** By our calculation, several 2027 surcharges rise more than the 5.9% average: international dimension-based additional handling by 9.3%, U.S. weight-based additional handling by 7.6% and the residential delivery-area surcharge by 6.1%. The residential delivery surcharge rises 5.8%, oversize charges for Home Delivery 5.5% and the International Ground inbound processing fee 5.7%. For shippers with heavy, bulky or residential volume, surcharges can push the effective increase above the average.

**Four years of compounding.** FedEx has now applied a 5.9% average increase four years running, for 2024, 2025, 2026 and 2027. Compounded, that is about 26% above 2023 list rates, by our calculation, before surcharges and before any contractual discounts.

## What it means for your parcel spend

The table below shows how the same 5.9% headline lands differently depending on your mix. These are illustrative profiles, not forecasts.

| Shipper profile | Main exposure | Likely effective increase |
|---|---|---|
| Light B2C packages, mostly residential Ground | Ground 1–5 lb rate plus residential surcharges | Above the headline |
| B2B, commercial addresses, mixed express | Overnight and 2Day rates | Near the headline |
| Heavy or oversized items | Handling and oversize surcharges | Above the headline |
| Transit-tolerant, using Express Saver | Express Saver rate | Below the headline |

## What procurement teams should do

- **Model your own mix.** Run last year's shipments through 2027 rates and surcharges to get your real increase.
- **Negotiate surcharges, not just discounts.** Residential, delivery area and handling fees are often negotiable in larger contracts.
- **Shift service levels where possible.** Move transit-tolerant shipments from 2Day to Express Saver or Ground.
- **Go paperless.** Make sure trade documents and airbills are electronic to avoid the new fees.
- **Get competing bids.** Use UPS's 2027 announcement and regional carriers as leverage.

## The bigger picture

Carriers have made annual increases near 6% routine, and surcharges keep growing as a share of the bill. The best defense is data: know your shipment profile, negotiate the fees that matter most to it, and re-bid regularly.
