## Why it matters

Outsourced fulfillment providers are consolidating. Stord has acquired networks from Pitney Bowes, UPS and Ceva Logistics and now has fresh capital to expand. When your 3PL grows quickly, your contract needs to protect service levels, data and your ability to switch.

## The details

Atlanta-based Stord closed a $400 million credit facility led by Citi on Oct. 8, with Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, First Citizens, Citizens and KeyBank participating. It closed oversubscribed at nearly double its original target. Combined with a $250 million Series F in May that valued the company at $3 billion, Stord says it has $650 million in equity and credit capacity this year.

The funding will expand the fulfillment network, accelerate robotics and fund AI development. Stord runs nearly 100 fulfillment locations, serves more than 1,000 brands and processes more than $15 billion in gross merchandise volume a year. It says revenue run-rate is approaching $1 billion.

Its growth has come largely through acquisitions: ProPack Logistics and Pitney Bowes' e-commerce fulfillment unit in 2024, Ware2Go from UPS in May 2025 with 21 warehouses, and Shipwire from Ceva Logistics in January with 12 fulfillment centers. Stord also named Bill Zerella, previously CFO of ACV Auctions, as CFO, and Mark Wayland, previously CRO at Box, as chief revenue officer.

## What procurement teams should do

- **Review change-of-control clauses.** Fast-growing providers may be acquired or restructured. Make sure your contract addresses assignment and your right to exit.
- **Pin service levels to sites.** Specify the facilities serving you and require approval before inventory moves to a new site.
- **Protect your data.** Confirm ownership and portability of order, inventory and customer data, especially as providers build AI on top of it.
- **Benchmark periodically.** Use renewals to compare pricing and service against other 3PLs, rather than auto-renewing with a provider whose network has changed.

## What to watch next

- **More acquisitions.** Fresh capital makes further network purchases likely; check if your current 3PL could be a target.
- **Robotics rollout.** Ask how automation changes service levels and pricing at the sites that serve you.
- **Contract renewals.** Use upcoming renewals to confirm site commitments and exit terms.

## The bigger picture

Capital is flowing to fulfillment platforms that combine networks, software and automation. That can mean better service and technology for shippers, but also more concentrated supplier markets. Procurement should manage 3PLs as strategic suppliers with clear exit paths.
