---
term: "General rate increase (GRI)"
short: "An annual increase in a carrier's published base rates, expressed as an average across lanes, services and weights."
related: ["Fuel surcharge", "Accessorial charges"]
---

Parcel and LTL carriers announce GRIs every year. Your real increase depends on your shipment mix and contract structure, so re-rate your own shipments rather than relying on the headline percentage.
