---
term: "Fuel surcharge"
short: "A charge added to freight rates to cover changes in fuel cost, usually indexed to the weekly U.S. average diesel price."
related: ["General rate increase (GRI)", "Landed cost"]
---

Truckload surcharges are commonly calculated per mile as (diesel price minus a base price) divided by an assumed miles per gallon. LTL and parcel carriers usually charge a percentage from a published table.
