---
term: "Country of origin"
short: "The country where a product was made or last substantially transformed, which determines the tariffs it owes."
related: ["Section 301", "USMCA", "Landed cost"]
---

For tariff purposes, origin is not where goods ship from. Assembly that changes a product's name, character or use can confer origin; simple repackaging cannot. Origin drives Section 301 exposure and USMCA eligibility.
