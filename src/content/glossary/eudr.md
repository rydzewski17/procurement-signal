---
term: "EUDR"
short: "The EU Deforestation Regulation, which from Dec. 30, 2026 requires companies to show that cattle, cocoa, coffee, palm oil, rubber, soy and wood products sold in the EU are deforestation-free."
related: ["Due diligence statement (DDS)", "Country of origin"]
---

Compliance requires plot-level geolocation data from the point of production.
