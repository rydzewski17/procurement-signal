---
term: "Section 301"
short: "A provision of the Trade Act of 1974 allowing the U.S. Trade Representative to impose tariffs in response to a country's unfair trade practices."
related: ["Section 232", "Tariff exclusion"]
---

Section 301 underpins the China tariffs in place since 2018 and the 2026 forced-labor tariffs covering 60 economies.
