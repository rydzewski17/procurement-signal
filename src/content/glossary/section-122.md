---
term: "Section 122"
short: "A provision of the Trade Act of 1974 allowing a temporary import surcharge of up to 15% for up to 150 days to address balance-of-payments problems."
related: ["Section 301", "Section 232"]
---

A 10% Section 122 surcharge applied from Feb. 24 to July 24, 2026, after the IEEPA tariffs were struck down.
