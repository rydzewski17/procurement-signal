# Sources and beats

Search widely each run. These are starting points, not limits. Prefer primary sources for facts; use trade press to find stories.

## Beats and example searches

| Beat (topic) | Example searches |
|---|---|
| Tariffs & Trade (`tariffs-trade`) | tariffs news this week; Section 232 tariffs; Section 301 USTR; CBP tariff guidance; de minimis; reciprocal tariffs; EU trade measures; China export controls |
| Supplier Risk (`supplier-risk`) | supplier bankruptcy Chapter 11 manufacturer; plant shutdown supply disruption; port strike; Red Sea shipping; semiconductor shortage; recall supplier |
| Commodities (`commodities`) | steel prices this week; aluminum price; copper price; Brent crude; diesel prices EIA; container freight rates Drewry; PMI prices index |
| Proc-Tech (`proc-tech`) | procurement software funding; source-to-pay acquisition; spend management startup raises; procurement platform launch; Coupa / SAP Ariba / Ivalua / Zip / Fairmarkit / GEP / Oracle procurement news |
| AI in Procurement (`ai-procurement`) | AI agents procurement; autonomous sourcing; AI negotiation suppliers; generative AI procurement survey |
| Public Sector (`public-sector`) | federal procurement FAR overhaul; GSA contract award; DoD contract award; public procurement EU directive; government contracting rule |
| ESG & Regulation (`esg-regulation`) | UFLPA entity list; CSDDD; CSRD; EU deforestation regulation EUDR; conflict minerals; supplier due diligence law |
| People & Careers (`people-moves`) | appoints chief procurement officer; new CPO named; procurement salary survey |

## Trade and industry press (to find stories)

- Supply Chain Dive — supplychaindive.com
- Spend Matters — spendmatters.com
- Procurement Magazine — procurementmag.com
- CPO Strategy — cpostrategy.media
- Supply Management (CIPS) — cips.org/supply-management
- Logistics Management — logisticsmgmt.com
- Sourcing Journal — sourcingjournal.com
- FreightWaves — freightwaves.com
- Industry Week — industryweek.com
- Federal News Network — federalnewsnetwork.com
- Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, Financial Times, CNBC (business/markets)

## Primary sources (to verify facts)

- Federal Register — federalregister.gov
- USTR press releases — ustr.gov
- U.S. Customs and Border Protection — cbp.gov
- Commerce Department / BIS — commerce.gov, bis.gov
- SAM.gov and USAspending.gov (contract awards)
- Defense.gov contract announcements — defense.gov/News/Contracts
- GSA — gsa.gov
- EIA (energy prices) — eia.gov
- BLS Producer Price Index — bls.gov/ppi
- ISM Manufacturing PMI — ismworld.org
- European Commission trade — policy.trade.ec.europa.eu
- Company newsrooms and SEC filings (sec.gov/edgar)
- PR Newswire / Business Wire (company announcements)

## Skip

Webinars, sponsored content, vendor "thought leadership", awards, minor feature updates, listicles, anything paywalled that you can't verify elsewhere.
